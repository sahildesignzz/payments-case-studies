Idea 003 · Pending at the counter

“Pending” at a shop counter makes people pay twice.

The problem

Your phone says pending. The shopkeeper’s soundbox says nothing. With a queue behind you, you pay again, and the first payment goes through later anyway.

The solution

  1. 1
    Show what’s known. Money left your bank ✓. Waiting for theirs.
  2. 2
    Show the shopkeeper. A big reference number they can match.
  3. 3
    Stop the second payment. One warning before paying the same person twice.

Uses only data the app already has. Nothing guessed.
