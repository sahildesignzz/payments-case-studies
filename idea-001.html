Idea 001 · Scan on open

Most of the time, I open a payment app just to scan a QR.

Yet every time

  1. Open
  2. Tap Scan
  3. Wait for camera
  4. Aim
  5. Pay

~90% of my opens, by my own estimate.

The idea

Let the home screen scan for me. Its blurred background is the camera, so there’s no waiting. When a QR shows up, it offers to pay.

Try it →

Allow the camera, then point it at any UPI QR.
